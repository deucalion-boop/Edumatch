1 About This Manual

EduMatch is a role-restricted school learning and records platform. This manual explains the actions that each authorized user can perform and the workflows that connect account administration, school records, classroom content, assessments, attendance, and student recommendations.

Objectives

  • Help first-time users sign in and navigate without assistance.
  • Define the correct responsibilities and limits of all five roles.
  • Provide repeatable steps for common operational tasks.
  • Explain warnings, approvals, and record-protection rules.
  • Give evaluators a source-aligned reference to the implemented system.

Scope and terminology

The manual uses the labels displayed in the application. A class is a teacher-managed subject offering. A section is an administrative student grouping with a school year and capacity. A lesson is teacher-published PDF content. An assessment may be an activity, challenge, quiz, or exam depending on its configured type.

2 EduMatch System Overview

EduMatch brings role-based account management, classroom operations, academic evidence, and recommendation reporting into one web application.

1Accounts and assignments→2Classes and learning content→3Assessments and attendance→4Grades and progress→5Recommendation evidence

Implemented functional areas

Identity and security

Username/password sign-in, math CAPTCHA, optional login verification controls, password recovery, forced password change, sessions, role guards, rate limits, login attempts, and audit records.

School structure

Grade 10 sections, school year, capacity, active/archive state, strand, departments, subject assignments, advisory assignments, and class codes.

Learning operations

PDF lessons, lesson attachments, class announcements, assessments, manual or AI-assisted question generation, draft/turn-in workflows, review, and scoring.

Evidence and reporting

Attendance, results, grading-period records, learning completion, role analytics, archived records, exports with approval, and strand-fit recommendations.

Data and permission model

Backend routes apply authentication first and then enforce a single permitted role. The database separates users, sections, subjects, enrollments, lessons, assessments, submissions, attendance, recommendations, notifications, sessions, settings, export approvals, announcements, invitations, registry records, and audit logs.

No invented modules. EduMatch does not implement separate administrator pages named School Year Management, Department Management, or Strand Management. School year is stored and controlled through sections and year-end archiving. Department and strand data are assigned in user, teacher, registry, section, and recommendation workflows.

Before first use

  1. Confirm that your name, username, email address, role, department, subject, section, and strand assignment are correct with school personnel.
  2. Open the official EduMatch URL supplied by the school. Do not use links from unknown senders.
  3. If you received an invitation or registration link, complete it before attempting ordinary sign-in.

  5. Allow pop-ups or downloads only when you intentionally request a report or file.

File safety. Upload only school-authorized files. Check filenames and content before submission; do not upload credentials or unrelated personal data.

4 Sign In and Navigation

Sign in

  1. Enter the username assigned to your account.
  2. Enter your password. Use the eye control only when no one else can see your screen.
  3. Select Keep me signed in on this device only on a private, trusted device.
  4. Solve the displayed math security question and select Verify. Refresh the question if it cannot be read or loaded.
  5. Select Sign In. Complete email verification or a required password change if the system presents either step.

Recover access

  1. Select Forgot password.
  2. Provide the registered account information requested by the form.
  3. Open the school-approved reset message and follow the time-limited link.
  4. Create a new unique password, then return to sign in.

Common navigation

  • The left sidebar opens role pages. On small screens, select the menu button.
  • The bell opens notifications where implemented.
  • The question-mark control starts the guided tour on student and teacher pages.
  • The gear opens Profile, Settings, and Logout.
  • Search and filter controls change only the visible list unless a Save, Apply, or Export action is selected.

4.1 Password Recovery Reset and Required Change

Request a reset

  1. Open Forgot password from Sign In.
  2. Enter the email address stored on the EduMatch account.
  3. Submit once and wait for the neutral response. The system avoids revealing whether an address belongs to an account.
  4. Open the most recent reset email from the approved sender.
  5. Use the reset link before it expires.

Set the new password

  1. Confirm that the reset page accepts the token. If it reports invalid or expired, request another link.
  2. Enter a new password containing 8 to 16 characters with the required uppercase, lowercase, number, and other displayed checks.
  3. Enter the same password again.
  4. Submit, wait for confirmation, and sign in with the new password.

Required first-login change

Accounts created with temporary credentials can set forcePasswordChange. EduMatch redirects every protected route to this page until the user verifies the temporary password and successfully creates a permanent one.

Security. A password reset or change invalidates assumptions about older credentials. Review active sessions afterward and revoke devices you do not recognize.

4.2 Invitations Registration Links and Account Activation

EduMatch has several controlled onboarding paths. The link determines the allowed role, assignment context, and registration form; users cannot promote themselves by changing a URL or form value.

LinkIssued byPurpose
Teacher invitation or private linkAdministrator or Head TeacherCreate an assigned teacher account; teacher invitation reuses the Login activation interface.
Student registry linkHead Teacher or authorized registry workflowVerify the registered student, email code, username, and password.
Class registration linkTeacherVerify a named student and create an account already linked to the intended class workflow.
Administrative private registration linkAdministrator or authorized Secretary workflowActivate a permitted staff role using the signed link context.

Student registry activation

  1. Open the exact private link supplied by school personnel.
  2. Enter the registered first and last name to verify the intended student record.
  3. Enter the student email and request the six-digit verification code.
  4. Enter the code before it expires and select Verify email.
  5. Choose an available username of 3 to 30 characters, beginning with a letter or number and using only letters, numbers, periods, underscores, or hyphens.
  6. Create and confirm an 8-to-16-character password that meets all displayed requirements.
  7. Create the student account and sign in.

Class-link activation

  1. Open the teacher-provided class registration link.
  2. Verify first and last name against the invitation.
  3. Confirm the displayed class/teacher context before continuing.
  4. Enter a Gmail address where the form requires Gmail, choose an available username, and create the password.
  5. Submit once and continue to the assigned student workspace.

Link handling. A private link is a credential. Do not forward it, publish it, edit its token, or reuse it for another person. Ask the issuer for a replacement when it is expired, revoked, already used, or assigned incorrectly.

4.3 Notifications Tours Search Filters and Responsive Navigation

Notifications

  1. Select the bell in the header.
  2. Read the newest items first. Student notification panels can mark all as read; teacher panels can clear displayed notifications according to their implementation.
  3. Select a notification action only after confirming its class, assessment, enrollment, or announcement context.
  4. Close the panel with its close control or by selecting outside it.

Guided tours

Teacher and student layouts implement role-specific tours. The question-mark button starts or restarts the tour. Use Next, Back, Skip, and Finish. The preference is stored so the application can avoid showing the tour repeatedly; it can be restarted later.

Search filter sort and pagination behavior

  • Search generally matches the text named in its placeholder; it does not modify a record.
  • Multiple filters combine. Clear filters before concluding that a record is missing.
  • Pagination changes the visible page, not the number of matching backend records.
  • Refresh re-requests authorized data and can discard unsaved filter-dependent state.
  • Export uses the active scope described beside the export action.

Mobile and narrow screens

  1. Select the menu button to open the sidebar.
  2. Choose a route; the drawer closes after navigation.
  3. Use horizontal table scrolling when a responsive card alternative is not displayed.
  4. Close overlays with their close button or the shaded backdrop.

Unsaved work. Before switching routes, closing a modal, refreshing, or signing out, save drafts and completed forms. EduMatch cannot preserve every partially entered form.

5.1 Administrator Dashboard and Analytics

Review platform status

  1. Select Overview.
  2. Read the metric cards for students, teachers, head teachers, secretaries, lessons, and assessments.
  3. Use the Day, Month, or Year control on role-growth trends when available.
  4. Scroll through analytics panels to compare role creation, activity, content publication, and other returned system metrics.
  5. Treat analytics as monitoring evidence; use User Management or Audit Logs to investigate a specific account or event.

Interpretation. A zero value can mean no matching data, an empty environment, or a failed/unauthorized data request. Refresh and verify filters before drawing conclusions.

5.2 Administrator User and Account Management

Find and inspect a user

  1. Open User Management.
  2. Filter by role or status and search by name, email, or username.
  3. Open a user action menu or profile view.
  4. Review identity, role, assignment, account status, learning progress, subjects, activity, and timestamps that the page provides.
  5. Close the profile without saving if no change is needed.

Create or invite an account

  1. Select the add/create action.
  2. Choose the supported role. The implementation provides private registration links for secretary, teacher, and head teacher onboarding.
  3. Enter the required identity and role-assignment information. Teacher and head-teacher details can include department or subject assignments.
  4. Create the link or account.
  5. Copy the generated private link and deliver it only to the intended person through an approved channel.
  6. Revoke or delete an unused registration link from its link list.

Edit, message, invite, or remove

  1. Open the selected user’s actions.
  2. Choose Edit to change allowed profile, role, status, or assignment fields.
  3. Choose Send Message or Send Invite when the corresponding action is available.
  4. Review the confirmation carefully.
  5. For deletion, enter the current administrator password when requested and confirm only after checking the exact account.

Warning. Deletion can remove the account and related records according to backend rules. Prefer suspension or deactivation when the purpose is temporary access control.

5.3 Reports Export Approvals and Student Account Requests

Learning progress and user exports

User Management includes report/export operations for visible user and learning progress data. Apply filters first so the export scope matches the intended audience.

  1. Set role, status, search, or learning filters.
  2. Review the on-screen rows and totals.
  3. Select the relevant export action.
  4. Store the downloaded file in an approved school location and restrict access to authorized staff.

Archived PDF export approval

  1. Open Request.
  2. Filter by status, requester, type, date, filters, or note.
  3. Open the pending request and verify the requester and requested archived-record scope.
  4. Approve or reject the request and record a concise review note where provided.
  5. The secretary must consume an approved request from the Archived page before the authorization expires or is otherwise invalidated.

Student account requests

Student settings can submit account deactivation or deletion requests. Administrator backend controls support listing and reviewing these requests. Process them only after identity, policy, and record-retention requirements are satisfied.

5.4 Section School Year Department and Strand Administration

Create a section

  1. Open Manage Sections and select Add section.
  2. Enter the section name.
  3. Enter the school year in the required four-digit range format, for example 2026-2027.
  4. Set student capacity between the allowed minimum and maximum.
  5. Save, then verify the section card, status, enrollment count, available slots, and school year.

Maintain sections

  1. Search by section, grade, strand, or school year and filter by year or active/archive status.
  2. Use Edit to change supported details. Capacity cannot be reduced below constraints enforced by the backend.
  3. Use Archive to stop active use while preserving historical references; use Restore to reactivate.
  4. Use Delete only for an unreferenced section. The system rejects deletion when records still depend on it.

Where school year department and strand are managed

  • School year: stored on sections and used by the secretary’s end-of-school-year archive workflow.
  • Department: assigned through user, teacher, and registry workflows; head teachers supervise their assigned department.
  • Strand: appears in section/student assignment and recommendation evidence. The standardized current implementation targets Grade 10 records.

5.5 Announcements Audit Logs and Settings

Publish a school announcement

  1. Open Announcements.
  2. Enter a clear title and complete message, including dates and required action.
  3. Select one or more audiences: students, teachers, head teachers, or the supported role combination.
  4. Review the recipient-group count and select Post announcement.
  5. Confirm the announcement appears in Published announcements.

Investigate an event

  1. Open Audit Logs.
  2. Search by actor, action, module, target, or IP address.
  3. Apply action, module, result, role, or date filters shown by the page.
  4. Read the timestamp, actor, target, request information, and outcome.
  5. Move through pages without changing the original audit record.

Configure settings safely

System Settings groups security, maintenance, appearance, and session controls. Review the current value before changing it, save deliberately, and verify the success message. Cache clearing and maintenance mode can affect other users, so schedule and communicate those actions.

5.6 Administrator Profile Security Sessions and Maintenance

Update the administrator profile

  1. Open the gear menu and choose Profile.
  2. Review the current summary before editing.
  3. Choose an accepted image and remain within the administrator photo size limit of 2 MB.
  4. Edit display name, email, or username only when the change matches the official account identity.
  5. Select Reset to discard local edits or Save Profile to submit them.
  6. Sign out and back in if another page continues to show cached identity text.

Security settings

The administrator settings interface controls sign-in limits and automatic account protection according to the saved system security model. Change one policy group at a time, record the previous value, save, and verify the confirmation.

Active sessions

  1. Open Settings and review the current and other sessions.
  2. Compare device description, IP address, and last activity.
  3. Revoke a non-current session that is no longer trusted.
  4. Do not revoke the current session until unsaved administrative work is complete.

Maintenance and cache

  • Maintenance mode: communicate the window before enabling it and supply the intended maintenance message.
  • Clear cache: use the confirmation dialog and expect pages to request fresh data afterward.
  • Appearance: affects display preferences; it does not change stored school records.
  • Reset settings: restores displayed defaults only after the confirmed save/reset operation.

Login Attempts. The legacy /admin/login-attempts route redirects to Audit Logs in the current router. Use Audit Logs and its sign-in/security filters rather than documenting Login Attempts as a separate active page.

6.2 Secretary User Registration and Account Management

Create or register an account

  1. Open User Management.
  2. Choose the available Add User or private registration-link action.
  3. Select the permitted role and enter the required identity and school assignment details.
  4. For a link workflow, create and copy the private link; send it only to the intended user.
  5. For a direct account workflow, verify the returned result and invitation status.

Maintain an account

  1. Search by name, email, or username and narrow results by role/status.
  2. Open the record and review its profile before acting.
  3. Use Edit for permitted identity, role, department, subject, section, or status changes.
  4. Use Send Invite for an account that still needs activation, or Message for a direct administrative notice.
  5. Use Delete only with authorization and after checking dependent records.

Secretary account policy. Database migrations enforce a single secretary account. If a secretary already exists, creation of another may be rejected even when the form is available to an authorized operator.

6.3 Student Records Enrollment Assignment and Year End

Find and review a student

  1. Open Student Records.
  2. Search by the supported student identifiers.
  3. Filter by school year, department, grade, strand, section, or other displayed criteria.
  4. Open the student record to review enrollment, adviser/teacher assignment, section, recommendation progress, and archive status returned by the backend.

Register and assign students

  1. Start the student registration or import action available on the page.
  2. Enter or upload only the required student registry data.
  3. Choose the correct department, teacher/adviser, section, and related assignment fields shown by the workflow.
  4. Validate rows and resolve any rejected or incomplete assignment before submission.
  5. Complete registration and verify the student in Student Records.

End a school year

  1. Apply the exact school-year filter and review the affected students.
  2. Start End School Year only after school approval and record checks are complete.
  3. Read the confirmation, including the archive scope.
  4. Confirm the operation and wait for completion.
  5. Open Archived and verify the transferred historical records.

Warning. Year-end archiving is a high-impact record operation. Do not use it merely to hide inactive students. Correct individual assignments first and keep the school-year label consistent with section records.

6.4 Secretary Sections Archived Records and Export Approval

Secretary section creation, editing, archive/restore, capacity review, and deletion rules are the same as the administrator workflow in section 5.4.

Review and export archived records

  1. Open Archived.
  2. Filter by school year, department, grade, and search terms.
  3. Review the exact visible scope. CSV/Excel actions may download directly according to the implementation.
  4. For PDF, create an export-approval request with the current filters.
  5. Wait for administrator approval; refresh the request status.
  6. After approval, select the PDF export action. The system consumes the approval for the approved scope.

Protected export. Changing filters after approval can invalidate the intended scope. If the selected archive view changes, request approval again.

6.5 Secretary Profile Password and Appearance

Update the profile

  1. Open the gear menu and choose Profile.
  2. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image no larger than 5 MB when updating the photo.
  3. Edit the display name and username within the displayed rules.
  4. Confirm the role remains read-only as Secretary.
  5. Reset unwanted changes or select Save Profile.

Change password

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Enter the current password.
  3. Create a new password meeting every displayed check and enter it again.
  4. Submit once and wait for confirmation.
  5. Sign in again if the active session is ended by the security operation.

Appearance

Select the available light, dark, or system-following appearance option shown by the page and save/apply it. Appearance changes do not alter exports or other users’ themes.

7.2 Teacher Management Invitations and Academic Supervision

Invite or create a teacher

  1. Open Management and choose the teacher invitation/registration action.
  2. Enter the teacher’s identity and ensure the department/subject aligns with the head teacher’s scope.
  3. Create or send the invitation.
  4. Copy a private link only when required, and deliver it securely.
  5. Use the invitation list to resend, revoke, or remove links according to the displayed status.

Maintain a managed teacher

  1. Search for the teacher and open the management record.
  2. Review department, subject, section/advisory assignment, status, and related counts.
  3. Edit only supported assignment or profile fields.
  4. Open the teacher’s students, lessons, or assessments for supervisory review.
  5. Use the announcement action to communicate through the selected managed teacher when required.

Content and assessment supervision

The backend permits head teachers to list managed lessons and assessments, create a lesson tied to a managed teacher, create or update an assessment, and use AI generation when the AI service is enabled. Confirm teacher, class, subject, dates, scoring, and question content before publishing.

7.3 Student Registry

Register one student

  1. Open Student Registry and choose the individual registration or private-link action.
  2. Enter the required student identity and school details.
  3. Assign the correct teacher/adviser and section when the form requires assignment.
  4. Create the registration and provide the activation link through an approved channel.
  5. Verify activation and assignment in the registry list.

Import or register multiple students

  1. Open the bulk/import workflow if displayed.
  2. Use the provided schema or template exactly; do not rename required columns.
  3. Assign teacher/department/section information required by the current registry flow.
  4. Validate the preview and correct duplicate, invalid, or unassigned rows.
  5. Submit and retain the result summary for follow-up.

Assignment rule. The implemented registry migrations require teacher assignment for student import and support department-assignment workflows. An incomplete assignment can be rejected rather than silently accepted.

7.4 Lessons Assessments and Learning Materials

Review learning materials

  1. Open Lessons.
  2. Filter by teacher, class, subject, or status using the controls provided.
  3. Open the lesson record and verify title, teacher, assignment, publication details, and PDF availability.
  4. Coordinate corrections with the teacher or use the permitted management create/update action where the page exposes it.

Create a managed assessment

  1. Open the assessment area from Management.
  2. Choose the managed teacher and correct class/subject.
  3. Set the title, type, dates, instructions, scoring, and lesson link supported by the form.
  4. Create questions manually, or request AI generation if the status indicates that AI is enabled.
  5. Review every AI-produced question, option, answer, and point value. Edit or discard unsuitable output.
  6. Save/update the assessment and verify its status and teacher assignment.

AI warning. AI generation is an authoring aid, not an approval. The head teacher or teacher remains responsible for curriculum fit, correctness, fairness, privacy, and final publication.

7.5 Head Teacher Announcements Profile and Settings

Maintain leadership identity

  3. Edit only fields enabled by the form; department assignment can be controlled by higher-level account administration.
  4. Choose an accepted profile image when the control is available.
  5. Save and verify the updated header/profile summary.

Security and appearance

Enter the current password, create and confirm a new password meeting the displayed rules, and submit. Use Appearance to select the available role theme option. Use the account menu to log out after finishing.

8.2 Create and Manage Classes and Students

Create a class

Handle enrollment

  2. Review the student, requested class, and section/advisory context.
  3. Accept a valid request or reject an invalid request.
  4. Confirm the approved student appears in the correct roster.

Registration links and roster maintenance

  • Create private or reusable student registration links, copy them, and revoke/delete unused links.
  • Search advisory students separately from students enrolled through handled-class codes.
  • Open a class roster to view members and remove a student only from that subject enrollment when the action is permitted.
  • Edit a class name or delete a class only after checking content, assessments, enrollments, and historical records.
  • Post an announcement to a selected class from the class management workflow.

8.3 Upload Lessons and Learning Materials

Upload a lesson

  1. Open Activities and choose Lesson Upload.
  2. Enter the lesson title.
  3. Select the class and subject. Create the class first if none is available.
  4. Select Next and upload one clean PDF lesson plan through the implemented PDF workflow.
  5. Review filename, title, class, subject, and publication details.
  6. Submit and verify the lesson in Records > Lessons.

Manage an existing lesson

From Records, teachers can list lessons, update supported lesson details, download their own lesson PDF/attachments, and copy a lesson to other compatible classes. Students receive view access; student download endpoints deliberately deny direct lesson downloads.

PDF quality. Open the PDF before upload. Ensure text is readable, pages are correctly oriented, and the file contains no answer keys or personal information that students should not receive.

8.4 Create Assessments Manually or with AI

Open Assessment Creation

In Activities, expand the Activities menu and choose Assessment Creation. The same protected teacher module supports activities/challenges and exam-style assessments, including attachments, class assignment, lesson linking, question editing, and copying to other classes.

Manual question workflow

  1. Enter the assessment title, type, class/subject, instructions, availability, duration or due date, points, and any permitted attachments.
  2. Link one or more relevant lessons when the form provides lesson selection.
  3. Choose manual creation.
  4. Add each question and select the supported question type.
  5. Enter options where required, identify the correct answer, and assign points.
  6. Review totals, wording, accessibility, and answer correctness.
  7. Save or publish according to the displayed status controls.

AI generation workflow

  1. Confirm the AI status is available.
  2. Enter the topic, learning objective, difficulty, question count/type, or source context requested by the form.
  3. Request generation once; wait for the result.
  4. Review every question and remove duplicated, incorrect, biased, ambiguous, or out-of-scope items.
  5. Verify choices, correct answers, explanations, and point values.
  6. Save only the reviewed version.

Important. Do not send student personal data, confidential records, or unpublished answer keys as AI prompts. AI availability depends on server configuration and rate limits.

8.5 Assessment Management Results and Grading

Review activity submissions

  2. Read the student’s typed response, links, and attached files.
  3. Check submission state and timestamps.
  4. Enter the score and feedback allowed by the review form.
  5. Save the review and verify the published result.

Assessment weights and grades

Teacher routes provide per-subject assessment-weight retrieval and update. When the Records interface exposes weight controls, set category weights carefully and confirm the total required by the form. Student grade history separates official grades from progress/completion indicators.

8.6 Attendance and Announcements

Record attendance

  1. Open Records and choose Attendance.
  2. Select the date, class/section, and roster view. Advisory attendance uses the assigned advisory section; handled-class attendance follows subject enrollment.
  3. Mark each student using the supported attendance status.
  4. Review missing marks and save the record.
  5. Lock the attendance record only when it is final. A locked record may not accept ordinary edits.

Review attendance history

Use date, class, section, or status filters to locate a saved record. Confirm whether the view is advisory or handled-class before interpreting absent or missing students.

Announcements

The standalone page displays announcements addressed to teachers. Teachers also publish class announcements from the Students/class workflow through the protected subject-announcement endpoint. Select the correct class, write a concise message, post, and confirm its audience.

9.4 Take an Assessment or Exam

Before starting

  • Use a stable connection and a charged device.
  • Close unrelated applications and browser tabs.
  • Read the allowed time, attempt rules, and linked lessons.
  • Start only when you can finish within the available window.
  1. Open Activities and select the assessment.
  2. Review the preparation panel and select the start action.
  3. Enter full screen when the exam interface requests it.
  4. Answer each question. Use numbered navigation, Previous, and Next question to review.
  5. For written questions, enter a clear response in the text area.
  6. Use Review and submit on the final question.
  7. Check unanswered items, then select Submit assessment once.
  8. Wait for the confirmation; do not close or refresh during submission.

Exam safeguards

The implementation starts a server-side assessment session, saves progress, logs assessment activity, and can pause or protect exam content when integrity conditions are triggered. Do not switch windows, copy protected content, use unauthorized assistance, or attempt to manipulate the timer.

Connection loss. If the page remains open, wait briefly for synchronization. Do not start a second attempt. If the session cannot resume, record the time and contact the teacher.

10 Security and Logout

Security best practices

  • Use a unique password and never share it with classmates or colleagues.
  • Keep invitation, registration, class, and password-reset links private.
  • Do not select Keep me signed in on shared or public computers.
  • Verify the EduMatch address before entering credentials.
  • Lock the device when stepping away.
  • Review active sessions and revoke unfamiliar devices.
  • Export only the minimum necessary records; store and transmit them through approved school channels.
  • Do not include student personal data in AI prompts.
  • Report unexpected role access, missing records, or suspicious messages promptly.

  1. Finish or save current work.
  2. Select the gear/account menu in the header.
  3. Choose Logout.
  4. Confirm that the sign-in page appears.

12 Frequently Asked Questions

Can an administrator directly manage every academic workflow?

No. Administrators govern accounts, sections, settings, approvals, announcements, analytics, and audits. Teacher and head-teacher routes own classroom content, assessment, roster, and attendance workflows.

Where is school year managed?

On section records and through the secretary’s year-end archive workflow. There is no separate school-year page.

Where are department and strand managed?

They are assignment fields in account, teacher, registry, section, and recommendation workflows, not standalone pages.

Why can a student view but not download a lesson?

The student download endpoints intentionally deny downloads. The built-in reader provides controlled access and progress tracking.

Is AI assessment output automatically correct?

No. A teacher or head teacher must review and edit every generated question before use.

Why is an enrollment request pending?

The teacher has not yet accepted or rejected the class-code request.

Can a teacher see students from another section?

Yes, when those students are approved in a handled subject class. Advisory attendance remains tied to the assigned advisory section.

Can archived records be exported to PDF immediately?

No. The secretary requests approval; the administrator reviews; the secretary then consumes the approved authorization for the matching scope.

What happens to a saved activity draft?

It remains editable and is not a final turn-in until the student submits it.

Who decides a student’s strand?

EduMatch presents evidence-based recommendation information. School policy and authorized personnel make the final decision.
